# Roadmap

This roadmap records the course project state reached by LAMBA Team 22. All five Sprints are complete. Future product development is outside the completed academic scope and must be planned separately by the customer or future maintainers.

## Sprint 1 — MVP v1 Foundation

**Dates:** 15.06.2026–21.06.2026  
**Status:** Completed

### Sprint Goal

Deliver the first integrated mobile flow so a car owner can create an account, create a basic digital vehicle profile, and navigate between the main product screens.

### Delivered outcome

- registration and sign-in foundation;
- Android-to-backend authentication integration;
- initial digital twin creation flow;
- core mobile navigation;
- shared UI components and design tokens;
- backend, PostgreSQL, and Alembic foundation.

---

## Sprint 2 — AI Assistant, Expenses, and Quality Stabilization

**Dates:** 22.06.2026–28.06.2026  
**Status:** Completed

### Sprint Goal

Integrate the AI assistant into the main product flow, extend car-history and expense functionality, and improve testing and quality documentation.

### Delivered outcome

- AI assistant chat and record-creation integration;
- expense, maintenance, and repair forms;
- history and timeline improvements;
- backend AI configuration and validation;
- registration response-time testing;
- quality requirements, testing documentation, and CI foundations.

---

## Sprint 3 — MVP v2 Statistics, Feedback Response, and Documentation

**Dates:** 29.06.2026–05.07.2026

## Sprint 4 — Week 6 Trial Increment

**Dates:** 06.07.2026–12.07.2026  
**Status:** Completed  
**Sprint size:** 61 Story Points  
**Trial release:** [v0.3.1](https://github.com/ernest0507/LAMBA_Team-22/releases/tag/v0.3.1)

### Sprint Goal

Deliver QR receipt processing, trip mode, achievements, and a customer-trial increment that could be evaluated before the final handover Sprint.

### Delivered outcome

- trip start/finish flow and mileage updates;
- foreground trip tracking and point synchronization;
- QR receipt scanning and backend integration;
- achievement screen and backend persistence;
- logout endpoint;
- assistant memory and breakdown-handling fixes;

### Resulting Week 6 follow-up scope

Customer feedback identified the need for:

- duplicate receipt protection;

- improved vehicle and achievement visuals;

- final deployment and maintenance documentation;
- final source archive, APK, and transition confirmation.

## Post-course status

The academic roadmap is complete. The repository records the final product and transition state reached by the end of the course and does not make commitments for unapproved post-course features.

Possible future development, production deployment, maintenance, support, monitoring, backups, and release work are customer-owned decisions and must be planned separately from the completed Assignment 6 scope.
