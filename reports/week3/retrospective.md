# Week 3 Sprint Retrospective

## What went well

1. The team connected the backend to the product foundation, which moved MVP v1 closer to a working end-to-end application instead of only a frontend prototype.

2. The team updated the application design and improved the visual direction of the interface. This helped make the product clearer and more consistent for customer review.

3. The team received positive and useful feedback from the customer. The feedback helped confirm the general product direction and clarified which parts of the application should be improved next.

## What did not go well

1. Synchronization between the frontend and backend teams was not strong enough. Some work streams were developed separately, which made integration and planning harder.

2. The team initially planned too many tasks for the Sprint. As a result, the scope had to be adjusted during the Sprint and some plans changed while the work was already in progress.

3. One team member became unavailable and stopped responding during the Sprint. This created additional uncertainty and required the team to reconsider task distribution.

## Action points

1. In the next Sprint, the team will redistribute part of the workload to reduce dependency on unavailable members and make responsibilities clearer.

2. The team will hold daily short synchronization meetings between the frontend and backend teams to check progress, discuss blockers, and keep integration work aligned.
