# LLM Usage Report

During Week 2, LLM tools were used to support documentation and language-related tasks.

The team used ChatGPT to translate the customer meeting transcript into English, clean the wording for readability, and correct grammar and punctuation without changing the meaning of the original discussion.

ChatGPT was also used to help prepare the customer meeting summary and to improve the clarity of written formulations in repository reports. This included restructuring text, making descriptions more concise, and checking that the documents followed the required assignment format.

The team has used the AI tools in Figma to make the prototype design using references and drafts 

The team has used AI assistant to get consultation about the frontend development process 

All AI-assisted outputs were reviewed and adjusted by the team before being added to the repository. The LLM was used as a writing and editing assistant, while final decisions about content, meaning, and submission were made by the team.
